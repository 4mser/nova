# Music for the NOVA video

`nova-musica.mp3` is the background track of the presentation video (`video/nova-pc.mp4` and `video/nova-movil.mp4`),
on its own, without NOVA's voice: 249.2 s, aligned frame by frame with the video (it comes in at 2.6 s, is extended by
splicing from second 44 of the track with a 3 s crossfade, and closes with a 3.5 s fade-out). In the video it also
dips under the voice (ducking); here it plays at full volume.

- Song: "Future Technology", by The_Mountain

- Use: inside the video and as editing material. It is not published on the site (`.vercelignore`).
