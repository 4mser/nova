# Full-length videos

NOVA's four long videos (`nova-pc.mp4` and `nova-movil.mp4`, in English and Spanish, 230 MB in total) are not
committed to this repository, to keep it light. They are published on the website
([nova-spaceapps.vercel.app](https://nova-spaceapps.vercel.app), and `/es/` for Spanish) and kept in a separate
private repository. The short clips used by the site are included here.
