Body scan

Point the camera at a crew member. Nova finds their body, fits the reference anatomy to their proportions and shows their organs with today's twin data. NOVA Station sees the scan live.

Runs on board: the vision models and the anatomy load from the ship. Organ positions are estimated from the body's landmarks and a reference atlas (BodyParts3D, HuBMAP · CC BY 4.0), not imaged. Demo, pending flight surgeon review.
