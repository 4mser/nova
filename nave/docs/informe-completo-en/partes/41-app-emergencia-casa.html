What keeps working even if the link drops

The functions a person depends on in a bad moment live inside the phone: the emergency protocols, messages from home already received and the private log. When there is a link, the phone adds live vitals, NOVA’s voice and the surgeon’s log.

  Emergency on the phone
in use

The ship’s 13 protocols also live inside the app (src/emergencia/protocolos.json), so they work without the ship and without internet. With a link, the guide shows the person’s vitals, speaks with NOVA’s voice (/v1/decir) and logs each step (/v1/bitacora). CPR has a haptic metronome at the step’s rhythm. Steps and voice in Spanish.

  Home and widget
in use

Family photos and messages are uploaded from Earth and arrive once the light delay has passed. The phone keeps the inbox to view without a link, replies with text or a photo (/v1/casa/respuesta) and shows the latest family photo in an Android home-screen widget, even with the app closed.

  Body scanner
partial

The “Body scanner” button in Today opens https://<nave>:8751/escaner.html with the crew member already chosen: the browser camera requires a secure page and the ship brings its own certificate. Tested with a simulated camera; a test on a real phone is still pending.

  Privacy
in use

The journal and everything written stay on the phone. Mood leaves only with “Share my day”. A cycle summary leaves only if the person chooses, and via /v1/privado goes only to the flight surgeon’s report, never to the crew screen.

The profile’s “The ship” card shows the core online, the distance in light-minutes and the last change sent from Earth. Below it, the private session with the psychologist on Earth: video arrives about 9 minutes late, so NOVA sends her the topics the person chose in advance.
