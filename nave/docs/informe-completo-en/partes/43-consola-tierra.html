Mission Control: what the flight surgeon sees, always minutes behind

The Earth console (ui/tierra/) is served by the same link at /tierra/. It represents the Earth side: everything it shows comes from the ship with the light delay, and everything it sends travels with that same delay, computed by the mission. Today, day 220, it is 9.9 minutes each way.

Earth console · crew status “as received”, 24 h alerts and the nightly report. Screenshot from November 9; the “Teach NOVA” panel on the next page was added later.

  What arrives

The state the ship sent 9.9 min ago (/v1/tierra/estado). Before the first delay has passed, the console says so: “first signal since link start”.

  Nightly report

24 h summary per person, alerts, emergency guides with their log, questions to NOVA with their sources, private summaries with consent and what NOVA needs to improve.

  What goes up

Core limit changes with their reason, signed packages for NOVA and messages from families. Each with its arrival time at the ship.
