The phone sees, the ship thinks

The scanner works like a headset tethered to a computer. The phone shows its own camera without waiting on the network and sends each frame to the ship, which runs the heavy models on its GPU and returns only the body points. If the ship does not answer, the phone does the vision with light models and says so on screen.

  No queue

Vision always processes the newest frame and drops old ones. An answer for frame N closes all earlier ones, and a frame with no answer in 400 ms is counted as lost. The hologram never falls behind by piling up work.

  The patient first

With two people in view, the patient is the one with the most horizontal torso: the one standing is the rescuer. Each phone has its own tracker (up to 4) so two cameras do not mix up the same person. The mask is cropped to the patient only.

  Self-calibrating gyroscope

Gyroscope axes differ between phones. NOVA learns them by comparing the measured rotation with how the body points moved, and only uses the sign when the relation is clear. It can be turned off from the station.

  Secure page on the ship network

The phone's browser only gives the camera to a secure page. The ship generates its certificate with rcgen (localhost, nova.local and its IP), stores it in ~/.nova-pc/tls and remakes it if the IP changes. The ship starts vision, restarts it if it crashes, and vision shuts down if the ship dies.
