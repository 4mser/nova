28  ·  Part III · Hologram by systems

Six body systems, each with its own data

In hologram style, the chosen system lights up in its color and the rest stays as a faint outline. A panel anchored to the organ shows the values and their curve over the last ten minutes, and says where each number comes from: measured, estimated or reported by the person.

Cardiovascular. The heart highlighted inside the rib cage.

Respiratory. Lungs with live SpO₂ and breaths per minute.

Skeletal. Skeleton with arms and estimated bone density (−2.1 %).

  Driven by the twin

The heart beats at the crew member's pulse and the lungs inflate at their breathing rate. It is the same live state as the rest of the ship.

  Color by level

An organ with an out-of-range value pulses in its level's color: lilac to watch, amber for attention and magenta for an Earth notice, the same levels as the flight core.

  Sweep on detection

When a body appears or you switch crew member, the hologram reveals itself from head to pelvis in 1.4 s. Curves are requested from /v1/historia every 5 s.

Renal and skeletal show fixed demo values for now: the ship does not yet receive urine tests or densitometry. The screen marks them as estimated.
