The mission and the crew on a single screen

The Mission view brings together what used to be spread out: where the ship is going, how long light takes, how much is left and how each person is. It uses the same orbital calculation from mision.rs (Standish J2000 elements and Hohmann transfer), now also drawn on screen.

Day 221 of 1,018, Nov 15, 2026. Solar system with the light signal traveling between the ship and Earth (10.0 min each way, 180 M km), mission figures on the right, timeline below and crew readiness with what arrives from their apps.

  Live solar system

Earth, Mars and the ship in their real position for the day. The cursor gives distances and delay. In a solar storm the particle waves are visible.

  The 1,018 days

Cruise to Mars, 500 days on Mars and return. It can be dragged to any day, back to Today or play the whole mission.

  Crew readiness

Heart, sleep, steps, HRV, mood, energy and alert. Each cell says where it comes from and how old it is; simulated data is marked. A click opens their Vitals.

  Figures in view

Mission day and percentage, Mars arrival, light to Earth and reply time, stable crew, how much of what is measured is real, 24 h cabin CO₂ against the 5,000 ppm limit and mission radiation against the 600 mSv career limit. The screen fits whole at 1600×1000 and 1440×900, no scroll.
