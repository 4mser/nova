31  ·  Part III · Control from the station

With the phone in the headset, the PC runs everything

Inside the headset nobody can touch the phone. So every scanner setting is also a button in the Live body scan view of NOVA Station, which also shows what the headset sees.

“Control the viewer” panel. What is lit is the state the phone reports, not the last click.

No new connection. The command rides in the response to the frame the phone already sends to the station, about 0.1 s after the click. The phone’s own buttons go through the same function.

The step, in both eyes. In the headset the procedure card sat in one eye only, with buttons nobody could touch. Now the step is drawn inside the image and says “answer on NOVA Station”.

The wound, with one click. In a hemorrhage, the wound is marked by clicking on the live image; the point reaches the phone as a coordinate of its own frame.

The headset at Space Apps. The phone sits inside the headset; the step is drawn in both eyes and the PC runs everything.
