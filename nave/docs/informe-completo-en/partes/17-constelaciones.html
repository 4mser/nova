17  ·  Mind · Constellations

Each crew member's week, drawn as their sky

Constellations is a new tab in Mind. It shows mood, energy and sleep for the last 7 or 14 days of those who share their day from the phone. The ship does not invent days: it reads one journal per person and, if someone does not share, their sky stays empty and says so.

Each person. Four compact constellations, the same as in each app: height is mood, each star's size is hours slept and the card compares the week with the previous one. Demo data.

  Two readings

Each person (default) shows each one's constellation with its palette and a dot that runs through the week. Together puts the whole crew on one chart, with a cursor per day, buttons to show or hide people and a table view with a fixed name column. Colors are a categorical palette validated for dark backgrounds, fixed per seat.

  Only what the person shares

In the app, “Share my day” sends the ship the last 14 days of check-in and sleep, each with its date. The ship stores them in a journal per person (/v1/diario) with the name of who wrote it. The simulator no longer invents mood, energy and sleep for real people. The card warns if the check-in was the same every day.

  What it is for

Mood dropping in several people at once points to something in the environment (sleep, workload, an event) more than to one person. It lets the crew and the psychologist on Earth talk with data; it classifies no one.
