32  ·  NOVA Station · The shared tablet

One tablet for everyone that turns personal with a card

NOVA Station is the fixed sick-bay tablet, standing at 30° on Luis’s station. Without a card it shows shared content and the emergency; with a crew member’s NFC card or QR it opens their space. It is an Expo app in kiosk mode that loads station.html from the ship and records the microphone so NOVA can listen (/v1/oir).

Orbit, no card. NOVA in the center and the crew around it with their vitals, the Earth–ship–Mars arc, radiation in the header, the emergency (no card needed) and the shared dock.

Twin, with a card. That person’s body today, region by region, with what NOVA is watching. Below, their actions: check-in, measure, ECG, home, kit and Ask NOVA.

Check-in. Sleep, mood and energy in three taps. Saved under whoever holds the session and only shared if they send it.

  Who is in front of the tablet

The session opens with the card or the QR and expires after 120 s idle; a ring in the header shows the time left. Everything logged in that window (check-in, measurements, kit withdrawals, messages home) is saved under that person.

  The emergency does not wait for a card

The emergency button is in the shared view and opens the step-by-step guide for anyone. In a solar storm the tablet turns amber, says “stay in the shelter” and opens the shelter guide for the whole crew.
