18  ·  Ask NOVA

An assistant that answers with ship data and cites its sources

The crew asks NOVA in writing or by voice. The ship builds the answer in /v1/preguntar: it first decides whether it is an emergency, computes priorities with fixed rules and passes the model only what it needs. The model writes; it does not decide medical steps.

Answer with citations. The numbers open the real page in the library.
