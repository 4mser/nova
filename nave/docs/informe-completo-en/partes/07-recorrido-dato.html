From the station to the screen in under a second

A real case from the demo: the simulator applies the “fainting” scenario and Ingrid's pulse drops to 34 beats per minute. This is how that value passes through each piece, top to bottom in time.

Reading · 1 Hz. One message per measurement with crew member, sensor, value, unit and quality from 0 to 1. Max 1 KB.

Classification. Plausible range per sensor; quality below 0.5 does not alert. Raising the level is immediate; lowering it takes 10 s in a row.

Silence · 120 s. If a sensor that has reported goes quiet, the core emits SIN_DATOS.

Status · 1 Hz. 4 crew members × 18 sensors with value, level and age; each alert goes out instantly.

Screens. NOVA PC gets the continuous stream; phones read /v1/estado every 5 s.

Earth. The report goes down with light delay; commands go up through a queue with a computed arrival time.
