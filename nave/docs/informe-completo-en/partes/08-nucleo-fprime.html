F Prime, the software that flew Ingenuity

F Prime is JPL's flight software framework, open under the Apache 2.0 license. NOVA includes it as a submodule in vuelo/lib/fprime and adds its own deployment, Nova/Nave: the standard subsystems plus four custom components, joined by typed ports.
