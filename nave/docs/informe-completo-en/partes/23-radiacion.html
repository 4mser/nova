Radiation, with its dose, its limit and a shelter

Radiation is one of the HRP's five hazards of spaceflight and it cannot be felt. radiacion.rs receives the reading from the detector Luis designed (POST /v1/radiacion). Until that detector exists, the ship estimates the rate from Curiosity's MSL RAD measurements and says so on screen.

Radiation, day 221. Ship cutaway with a 20 g/cm² hull and a shelter with 10 cm of extra water (OCHMO, p. 10; illustrative). Current rate 79 µSv/h simulated, 408 mSv accumulated, 68 % of the career limit.
