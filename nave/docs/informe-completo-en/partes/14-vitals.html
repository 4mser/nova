Each sign's history and what comes in the next 15 minutes

The Vitals view draws the history stored on board (1 Hz for 6 h and per minute for 30 days, on disk; windows of 10 min to 7 days) with the core's current thresholds on top, projects each sign forward and warns when something departs from what is expected.

Kenji's pulse, last hour. In lilac, the 15 min cone: 55 ±18 bpm, with a 14% chance of landing at the attention threshold (≤ 45).

Trend. Weighted least squares over that person's last 30 min, with more weight on recent data (15 min constant).

Cone. Projects a quarter of the chosen window, up to 15 min. Width is ±2 deviations and grows with the horizon.

Crossing probability. The chance of passing the attention threshold at the end of the horizon. Above 30% the cone turns amber.

Unexpected change. If the latest reading lands more than 3 deviations from what the trend expected, the panel marks it as unexpected change.

  Honest scope

Simple, explainable statistics on each person's data, in the spirit of world models: predict and act on the prediction. It is neither a learned model nor clinically validated. It runs only with readings from the last 30 s.
