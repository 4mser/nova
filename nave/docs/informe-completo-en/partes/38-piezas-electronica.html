Sensor · RC522 reader · 13.56 MHz

NFC reader

Each crew member taps their card here to open their space on the tablet. Any card works: the ship links it to a person with “Link a card”.

CardsISO 14443-A (MIFARE, NTAG); uses the serial number
Fits37 × 70 × 5 mm pocket in the NFC adapter
WiringSPI to the Arduino through the pocket slot
PathArduino → USB → bridge → POST /v1/nfc
Controller · sensor hub

Arduino Uno R3

Reads every station sensor and sends each reading to the ship. The core does not need to know the board: if the message meets the contract, it understands it.

ChipATmega328P · 16 MHz · 32 KB flash · 2 KB SRAM
I/O14 digital pins · 6 analog inputs · 5 V
LinkUSB serial to the bridge on the station computer
Contractestacion/CONTRATO.md and estacion/NFC.md
Sensor · AD8232 heart monitor

Heart board

Reads the heart of whoever is at the station. The ECG appears live on the tablet and the PC; the pulse goes to that person’s record and through the on-board thresholds. If an electrode comes off, the station says so instead of showing a false value.

BoardAD8232, single-lead ECG, 3 electrodes
Sampling250 Hz on A0; loose electrode on D2/D3
Bridgefinds each beat, pulse and HRV (RMSSD)
Sendsthe live wave, plus pulse and HRV every 2 s

Each part isolated in the Hardware view of NOVA PC; dimensions taken from Luis González’s Inventor assembly.
