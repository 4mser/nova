  Home: family also travels with light

Family texts and photos (tierra/casa.py) arrive with the light delay at the time of sending and only then appear on the phone and the “Home” widget. Replies wait for the next contact.

  Systems: the core in the crew's words

tierra.rs reads the F Prime GDS (:5080) every second and translates it with the deployment dictionaries. A command from Earth waits out the light delay before entering the core.
