Executive summary

An on-board doctor that doesn't wait for Earth.

On a trip to Mars, a consult with the flight surgeon takes 6 to 44 minutes round trip, and during solar conjunction the link drops for weeks. NOVA handles monitoring, detection and first response inside the ship. Vital signs flow through a core built on F Prime; the crew sees them on the screen, on their phone and over the patient's body in augmented reality; a local assistant answers from open medical sources. Earth receives a report every night and sends back signed updates.

Three principles

01 · All on board

No critical function depends on the cloud or on Earth. Models, library, voice and vision run on the same ship computer.

02 · Fixed rules

Thresholds live in the flight core and emergency steps come from a reviewable file. The model explains and assists; it does not decide.

03 · The person decides

Each crew member chooses what to share. Mood leaves the phone only if they share their day; what they write never leaves.
