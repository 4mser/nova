Thirteen fixed protocols, with the source in view

When someone writes or says an emergency, the ship answers with the written protocol, without going through the language model. The step-by-step guide asks, measures and decides with the crew member's real values, in English or Spanish.

  Fixed text

The ship recognizes the emergency (nova::emergencia) and returns the written steps and where the needed items are in the kit. What the crew reports overrides a sensor.

  Branching guide

Question, action, wait or close. Waits read the twin against the protocol thresholds. During compressions a metronome plays at 110 per minute.

  Log

Each answer is logged with a time and the Earth console reads it at /v1/bitacora. The fainting guide opens on its own if pulse and pressure drop together.
