13  ·  The ship screen

A screen for the crew, readable from two meters

NOVA PC is the ship's desktop window (Tauri) and the same interface served by the link on :8750. It receives the F Prime core status once per second over SSE and shows it in eight views. The Crew view is the home view: the four crew members side by side, with NOVA on the right.

Crew view, Nov 14, 2026, mission day 220. Pulse ring, rhythm and six vital signs per crew member. Kenji shows in amber for a mood of 2/5 in his check-in.

  NOVA cards

Each warning comes from fixed rules, not the model: what changed, what to do and the source. Low pulse and pressure together open the fainting guide. Acknowledge mutes it 2 min if the level does not rise.

  Fills any window

Designed for 1800×1000, escala.js scales it by a factor of 0.68 to 1.35: text grows with the window and the grid shares out the rest. Works on a laptop or on the module screen.

  On-board voice and hearing

Speaks with Kokoro sentence by sentence; if it fails, it uses the system voice, also offline. Listens with whisper.cpp: cuts after 0.9 s of silence and does not hear itself.
