30  ·  Part III · Procedures on the body

36 steps shown on the patient's body

The steps of the emergency guides can be followed with the body scan. For each step NOVA knows where it goes (a point on the fitted body), what to watch (the rescuer's hands, a timer, the twin's vitals) and when to speak. The text comes from the protocol; AR only places it. not tested with a rescuer

  Confirm and correct

With the hands 1.2 s on the point, NOVA says “Good. That is the spot.”. If they are close but off, it corrects with a body reference and the distance (“No. 4 centimeters toward the head”): after 0.8 s on a critical step and after 3 s on one that is not.

  Follow without nagging

If the hands stay still for 4 s away from the point or the step takes 1.5 times the expected time, NOVA redirects. It speaks at most one sentence every 3 s and never repeats the same one within 8 s.

  Waits decide with the twin

When a wait ends, NOVA reads the crew member's values against the protocol's own recovery thresholds, picks the branch and says why (“Recovered: Glucose 96”).

  Everything is written down

Each intervention is logged as instruct, confirm, correct, follow, decide or close, with who started it, and reaches the station live.
