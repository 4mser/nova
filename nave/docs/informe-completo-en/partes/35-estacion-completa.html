35  ·  NOVA Station · The assembled station

The whole station, measured from the real assembly

Luis modeled the station in Autodesk Inventor. The ship loads it from that same STEP assembly, converted to estacion.glb (8 parts, in meters), and places the real NOVA Station screen on it, still working inside the model.

Assembled. The tablet standing at 30° with the live screen, the NFC reader at the side and the station body that holds the Arduino and the heart board.

  Live labels

Each label gives the real state of its part: whether a card is present, whether the electrodes are on, whether the Arduino responds.

  One click, one part

A click isolates the part: the camera frames it and orbits around it next to its info card.

  Real screen

The model’s tablet shows the same as the physical tablet, and is only visible from the front: from behind, the tablet itself hides it.
