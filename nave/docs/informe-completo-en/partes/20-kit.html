20  ·  Medical kit

The medical kit as an object graph NOVA can walk through

On Mars there is no resupply. The medical kit lives on the ship as a live inventory: each supply with its quantity, drawer, expiry and the protocols that use it. NOVA reads it before naming anything, so it says where it is and how many are left.

Drawer A open, day 221. Advanced life support: the drawer slides out and releases its supplies as cards with their real photo; on the right, each supply with its quantity and expiry.

  3D rack

Six drawers in the Space Station style. They slide out on touch and release their supplies as cards with a real photo: 42 free Wikimedia Commons photos, credited.

  Emergency mode

Choosing an emergency lights up only the drawers and supplies of that protocol, with a thread of light from each card to its drawer.

  Autonomy

The day each supply runs out or expires, against day 259 (Mars) and 1,018 (return). Each use is logged in kit.json.

Ask the rack in plain words

  Scene graph

As in ConceptGraphs (Gu et al., 2023), /v1/kit/buscar passes the rack to the brain as a list of objects: what it is, what it is for, where it is and how many are left.

  The ship verifies

Only objects that exist in the rack count and the ship sets the real drawer. The screen opens it and NOVA says it out loud.
