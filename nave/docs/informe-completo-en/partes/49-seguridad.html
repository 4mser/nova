49  ·  Rigor · Clinical safety and privacy

Fixed rules where there is risk, and the person decides what is shared

  Clinical safety

Thresholds in the flight core, with two levels and 10 s of hysteresis to clear.

Verbatim emergency steps from a versioned file, with their source.

The language model gives no emergency steps or diagnoses.

What the crew reports overrides what the sensors say.

AR anatomy is an estimate and the screen says so: “organ positions estimated”.

  Privacy

Nothing goes to the internet: models, library, voice and vision run on the ship.

Mood and energy leave only with “Share my day”. The journal never leaves the phone.

The scanner stores no frames: the ship keeps only the last one in memory for the station.

/v1/privado takes consented summaries only to the flight surgeon.

The demo with an external model is declared on screen and sees only demo data.
