Three layers and one path: everything goes through the ship

NOVA Station combines a tablet, the physical station designed and built by Luis González, and a bridge on a nearby computer. None of the three stores its own data: the tablet shows, the Arduino measures and the bridge translates. What matters stays on the ship and in its F Prime core.
