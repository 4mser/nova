The same as the big screen, within arm’s reach

Each dock button opens a sheet with the same parts as NOVA PC: the kit’s 3D rack, the mission with the real light delay and the crew’s mind. The tablet stores nothing of its own: everything comes from the ship and goes back to it.

Medical kit. 3D rack, supplies with photos and “Take 1” under the session.

Mission. Ship position, day 221 of 1018 and 10 min of light to Earth.

Crew mind. Mood, energy and sleep of those who share them.
