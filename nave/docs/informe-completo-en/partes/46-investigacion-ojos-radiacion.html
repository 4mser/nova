Two tracks that already changed the ship

The team's work lives in research/ in the public repository. Each track states its status; the connections to the ship named here exist in the code.

  Eye health with computer vision · Nicolás Richardson

Eye monitoring with a camera only: blinking, PERCLOS, lower eyelid position and distance to the screen, compared with each person's baseline. A follow-up notice, never a diagnosis. Includes a background review with primary sources and verification tags, a phased plan and pytest tests.

  Active radiation detector · Luis González

Conceptual design (9-page proposal) with seven changes to make it viable in flight: ⁶LiF/ZnS:Ag screens for neutrons instead of ³He, a single Bonner sphere with detectors at six radii, CsI(Tl) or GAGG:Ce instead of NaI(Tl), a ΔE-E telescope for protons, a silicon LET spectrometer for heavy ions (no LET, no dose equivalent), a fast solar alert channel in the FPGA and SiPM handling under radiation. Budget of ~15–25 kg and 10–25 W, a simulation (Geant4, FLUKA, MCNP) and calibration plan, and a four-phase roadmap up to a demo flight in low Earth orbit.

On the ship: the station contract already defines its message (tasa_usv_h, alerta_spe, let_kev_um, neutrones_usv_h, protones_cm2_s) and the Radiation view shows each channel “waiting for hardware”. It is not built. Suggested physics review: Daniella Mora.
