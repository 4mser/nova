Two thresholds per side and a rule against false alarms

The health monitor compares each reading with two limits: attention, handled by the crew, and Earth notice, which calls the flight surgeon. The 31 limits are F Prime parameters: they live in prmDb, change by command and the screen draws them exactly as they are in the core.

Starting values (docs/umbrales.md), all “pending” until a physician validates them. HRV, resting pulse, steps, light, noise, cabin O₂ and temperature have no fixed threshold: they will be compared with each person's baseline.

  Estacion

Passive component at 1 Hz. Reads up to 256 messages per cycle without blocking, checks unit and range, and raises PERDIDA if the station is silent 15 s.

  MonitorSalud

Active component with a queue. A reading with quality below 0.5 is stored without alerting; an impossible value is dropped as a sensor error, not an emergency.

  Pantalla

Bridge to NOVA PC: sends the full status and each alert over UDP 50051 and receives what the crew enters by hand on 50052.
