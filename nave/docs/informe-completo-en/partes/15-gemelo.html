15  ·  Body twin

A 3D anatomical twin colored with today's data

Each crew member has a reference body that can be rotated, zoomed and opened by layers. Ten regions are colored by their status: live from the F Prime core, or estimated by the on-board model with NASA-published rates, and the card says which of the two it is.

Mara's twin, day 220. Skin, skeleton and organs on; on the right, the ten regions with today's value. These are reference bodies, not a scan of the person.

  Real, open anatomy
